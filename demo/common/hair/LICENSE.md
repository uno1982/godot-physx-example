# Hair demo: head and hairstyles

CC BY-SA (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

From Blender's "Hair Styles" demo file by **Daniel Bystedt**
(https://www.artstation.com/dbystedt), licensed CC BY-SA, published at
https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/ (`hair_nodes-female_hair_styles.blend`).
The source names the license without a version.

Changed from the original: two of its four hairstyles were exported from
their Blender hair curves to Alembic, and the head mesh to glTF, without
editing the grooms or the sculpt.

- `hair_long.abc`, `hair_curly.abc` -- the long and curly hairstyles.
- `head.glb` -- the head they're groomed on.

These files, and anything adapted from them, are under CC BY-SA, separately
from the rest of this repository (MIT). Scenes that only use them, such as
`demo/editor/hair/hair_styles.tscn`, stay under the repository's license.
